Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Dryyt ("Dryyt", "we", "us") is an order-tracking service that lets shops ("Owners") register customer orders and lets their customers ("Customers") follow the status of those orders. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and dispose of personal information, and the rights you have under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission ("NPC").

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • Dryyt is the personal information controller for account data (for example, your email, display name and password) and for data needed to run the service securely.
  • Each Owner is the personal information controller for the customer and order details it enters about its own Customers. For that data, Dryyt acts as the Owner's personal information processor and processes it only to provide the service to that Owner. Questions about how a shop uses your order details may also be sent to that shop.

4. Tracking codes and public pages

  • Anyone who has an order's tracking code can open its /track page and see the shop's name and address, the control number, the customer name, weight, notes, status and last-updated time. Treat your tracking code and claim slip like a receipt and share them only with people you trust.
  • An Owner's public store page shows the shop's queue (control numbers and statuses) to anyone; customer names, codes and notes are hidden from visitors who are not signed in.

5. Who we share it with

  • The Owner who registered your order sees the order and the customer details entered for it.
  • Service providers (processors) that host and protect the service, under agreements that require them to keep the data secure and confidential: Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting, database, file storage, real-time connections, and Cloudflare Turnstile bot protection).
  • Dryyt administrators, only when needed for support, security or to act on your request. Administrators can view an account as its user for troubleshooting; this access is limited to authorised personnel.
  • Government authorities, only when required by law or a lawful order.

6. Storage and cross-border transfer

Our service providers operate data centres in and outside the Philippines, so your information may be stored or processed abroad. Under Section 21 of RA 10173 we remain responsible for that information and use contractual and other reasonable means to ensure it receives a comparable level of protection.

7. How long we keep it

  • Ended orders are automatically deleted about 3 months after they were created. Open orders are kept until they are ended.
  • Accounts are kept while they are active, and deleted or anonymised within [30] days after a verified deletion request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 7 days and expired sessions are deleted daily.
  • Password reset links expire shortly after they are issued.
  • The "My Track" cookie expires after 90 days.

Data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised so that it can no longer identify you.

9. How we protect it

We apply organisational, physical and technical security measures as required by Section 20 of RA 10173, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic;
  • passwords stored only as salted, one-way hashes;
  • role-based access, so Owners see only their own orders and customers;
  • sanitising user-entered rich text to prevent malicious code;
  • bot protection on account forms, and automatic expiry of sessions and reset links.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to give rise to a real risk of serious harm, we will notify the NPC and affected individuals within 72 hours of knowledge of the breach, as required by NPC Circular No. 16-03.

10. Your rights as a data subject

Under Sections 16 to 18 of RA 10173 you have the right to:

  • Be informed whether and how your personal information is processed;
  • Access your personal information and obtain a copy of it;
  • Object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Rectification — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • Erasure or blocking — have data removed or blocked when it is no longer necessary, was unlawfully obtained, or you withdraw consent;
  • Data portability — obtain your data in an electronic, commonly used format;
  • Damages — be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your data;
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

You can update most account details yourself in your profile settings. For any other request, email our Data Protection Officer. We may ask you to verify your identity and will respond within [15] working days. For order details entered by a shop, we will coordinate with that shop as the controller of that data. These rights are free of charge, except where a request is manifestly excessive.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the National Privacy Commission — privacy.gov.ph, complaints@privacy.gov.ph.

11. Responsibilities of Owners

Owners who enter customer information into Dryyt must have a lawful basis to do so, must tell their Customers that their details will be recorded in Dryyt for order tracking, must collect only what is needed, and must honour their Customers' data subject rights. See also the Terms & Conditions.

12. Minors

Dryyt is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. Minors may only use the service with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a minor has provided us personal information without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. For significant changes we will give notice in the app before they take effect.
